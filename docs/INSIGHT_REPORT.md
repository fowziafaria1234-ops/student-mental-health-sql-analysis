# Student Wellbeing Insight Report

## Executive summary

Across the synthetic student cohort, average wellbeing is **70.3** and average stress is **45.9**. **Arts** has the highest programme-level average wellbeing, while **Engineering** has the lowest. Students in the longest stay cohort average **8.0 points** higher wellbeing than the newest cohort.

## Support engagement

**7.4%** of students have a recorded support engagement. This measure should not be interpreted as unmet need; it reflects only engagement within the synthetic model.

## Privacy approach

- Student identifiers are pseudonymous.
- Direct identifiers are excluded from the analytical layer.
- Small-cell suppression is demonstrated in SQL.
- Results are reported at cohort level.

## Recommendation

Use longitudinal term-level monitoring, investigate programme-specific differences and ensure support-access metrics are interpreted alongside wellbeing and stress outcomes.
